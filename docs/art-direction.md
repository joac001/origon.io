# Art Direction

Reference: dense temperate forest in the style of modern photoreal open-world games
(Pacific Northwest look). Origon builds its own look; it copies no game's assets.

Key traits from the reference image:
- Tall conifers and redwoods with thick reddish bark. Mid-size deciduous trees in between.
- Dense understory: ferns, bushes, broad-leaf ground plants, tall grass on slopes.
- Moss-covered rounded rocks and boulders. Fallen logs with moss and bark detail.
- Shallow clear creek: visible stony bed, refraction, soft ripples, reflections of the canopy.
- Light: warm sun filtered by the canopy. Strong dappled shadows on the ground. Visible light
  shafts in the haze. Cool blue-green ambient in shaded areas.
- Atmosphere: soft aerial perspective. Distant trees fade into haze.
- Palette: desaturated greens, warm browns and ochres, grey-blue stone.

Engine features this drives: CSM + soft shadows, volumetric fog, SSAO, instanced foliage with
wind, PBR with scanned materials, water shading with refraction, ACES-style filmic tonemap.
